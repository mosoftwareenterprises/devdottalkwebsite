# Volunteer Help

Thanks so much for helping out at {dev.talk}. This page is a quick guide for the main tasks to run a dev.talk event.

There are 2 kinds of volunteers we want: Physical volunteers at the events and Virtual Helpers (you can be both). Each type of volunteer can do different tasks.

## Physical Volunteers

### Checkin attendees

To help out at checkin time, please download the Luma Check-In app and sign in with your email address.
Once you are signed in, you should be able to see the event and access the checkin options.

- Luma checkin guide: [Check in guests for in-person events](https://help.luma.com/p/check-in-guests-for-in-person-events)

### Setup event space

- Arrive a little early so we can get the room ready.
- Help place signage and arrange seating where needed.
- Check that the speaker area is tidy and ready before doors open.

### Pack down event space

- Help clear the room after the event finishes.
- Pack away signs, badges, and any event supplies.
- Leave the space clean and ready for the venue team.

### Welcoming people

- Greet attendees as they arrive.
- Help new people feel comfortable and point them to the right area.
- Introduce people to each other where helpful to encourage networking.
